Research

Recent work includes diagnosing inference-time mode collapse in RL-trained Lean theorem provers (ICML 2026 AI4Math Workshop), PhD-level expert evaluation for QEDBench at ICML 2026, a pre-registered study of whether visual-reasoning post-training transfers to math, and RLHF infrastructure for interactive policy personalization (PIEFACE).

Inference-Time Diversity in RL-Trained Lean Theorem Provers: A Diagnostic Study
ICML 2026 AI4Math Workshop (Poster), First Author
arXiv / pdf / poster / code

RL-trained Lean provers mode-collapse at inference time, and a fixed schedule of tactic skeletons breaks the plateau.

Details

On miniF2F-test, doubling DeepSeek-Prover-V1.5-RL's sampling budget from k=32 to k=64 solves zero additional theorems. A fixed schedule of 15 tactic skeletons adds +12.3 ± 4.2 theorems across 3 seeds, and a controlled ablation shows the gain comes from tactic structure, not prompt diversity.

Negative Transfer is the Default: Visual Reasoning to Math on Qwen2.5-VL-7B
Research study, 2026

A pre-registered test of whether post-training on abstract visual reasoning (Raven's matrices) improves math.

Details

Eight matched-eval SFT and GRPO conditions were all negative on MATH-500 (base 66.8% → 44.0–61.6%); the damage traces to completion compression rather than format failure. Extended to from-scratch 162M pretraining, where a 1% formal-language (Dyck) warm-up cut tokens-to-threshold on compositional algebra by 12.3% against a scrambled control (3/3 seeds).
